## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. AUTHENTICITY-VALIDATOR-FOR-ACADEMIA-P-1-
Academic integrity and document verification platform that combines multiple checks (AI-content signals, plagiarism similarity, citations, grammar, certificate verification) into an authenticity score with a verification report.
- 🔗 **Repo:** https://github.com/Nishanth-1628/AUTHENTICITY-VALIDATOR-FOR-ACADEMIA-P-1-

### 2. AI-Based-Emergency-Crowd-Risk-Predictor
Browser-based crowd-safety monitoring dashboard prototype. Uses a simulated live feed and a heuristic risk engine (density, growth rate, bottleneck) to show zone-level risk, alerts, and risk trends.
- 🔗 **Repo:** https://github.com/Nishanth-1628/AI-Based-Emergency-Crowd-Risk-Predictor

### 3. Smart-Student-Digital-Safety-Guardian
Browser-based digital safety learning platform for students with password-strength checks, phishing-awareness exercises, screen-time tracking, incident recording, and safety guidance. Data is stored locally in the browser.
- 🔗 **Repo:** https://github.com/Nishanth-1628/Smart-Student-Digital-Safety-Guardian

### 4. Fake-emergency-alert-detection
Client-side emergency message verification app (SENTINEL-ALERT). Analyzes alert text for urgency, fear, authority, forwarding and formatting signals, shows a risk score with explanations, and stores scan history locally.
- 🔗 **Repo:** https://github.com/Nishanth-1628/Fake-emergency-alert-detection

### 5. movie-ticket-booking
Movie ticket booking repository.
